## Instructions

1. Cook the rice per the package directions and let it cool — ideally spread it out and
   refrigerate overnight, which keeps the grains separate in the wok.
2. Dice the chicken, carrot, and onion small; mince the garlic; slice the scallions.
3. Scramble the eggs in 1 tsp of the olive oil with a pinch of salt and pepper in a wok or
   large skillet over medium heat. Set aside.
4. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the chicken with salt, pepper, and 1 tbsp of the soy
   sauce until nearly cooked through, about 2 minutes.
5. Add the garlic, carrot, onion, and 2 tbsp of the butter. Stir-fry until the onions turn
   translucent, 2–3 minutes.
6. Add the scallions and stir 1 minute, then add the rice, the remaining salt and pepper,
   the remaining 3 tbsp soy sauce, and the sesame seeds. Toss to coat.
7. Push the rice out to the edges to make a well in the center and add the last 2 tbsp
   butter. Let it melt and the rice crisp undisturbed for about 2 minutes.
8. Return the eggs, stirring and breaking them into small pieces until distributed. Serve.

## Notes

Day-old refrigerated rice makes a real difference — fresh rice steams instead of frying.
For a vegan version, swap mushrooms for the chicken and sesame oil for the butter.
