## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C fan). Slice the peppers and red onion into strips and put
   them in a baking dish with half the fajita seasoning, salt, pepper, and the olive oil.
   Mix well.
2. Rub the remaining seasoning and the honey into the chicken thighs and season with salt
   and pepper. Lay them on top of the peppers and bake 30 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, make the flatbreads: season the yogurt with salt and pepper, then fold in the
   flour until a dough forms. Knead briefly and roll out thin on a floured surface.
4. When the chicken is done, take it out and let it rest. Cook the flatbreads in a hot dry
   pan, about 30 seconds per side, until lightly charred.
5. Slice the chicken and toss it with the peppers. Pile into the flatbreads and top with
   sliced avocado, crumbled feta, and sour cream. Finish with a squeeze of lime.

## Notes

Flour is 200 g (about 1 3/4 cups) and yogurt 200 g (about 3/4 cup plus 2 tbsp) — the
flatbread dough is a 1:1 ratio by weight, so a scale is easier than cups here. Chicken is
400 g, roughly 4 thighs.
