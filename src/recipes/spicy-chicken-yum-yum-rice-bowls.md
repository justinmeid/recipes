## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
2. On a large sheet pan, toss the thinly sliced chicken with 1 tbsp of the soy sauce and
   1 tbsp of the orange juice. Season with pepper. Sprinkle the cornstarch over the chicken,
   toss lightly, then drizzle with the olive oil. Bake 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, whisk the remaining 5 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp orange juice, the honey, chili
   paste, garlic, and ginger in a bowl.
4. When the chicken is ready, pour two-thirds of the sauce over it. Toss and bake another
   5 minutes, until cooked through. Switch the oven to broil and broil 1–2 minutes, until
   the chicken chars at the edges. Spoon over the remaining sauce if you like.
5. Make the yum yum sauce: combine the mayo, ketchup, Worcestershire, seasoned salt, and
   cayenne in a bowl. Taste and adjust the cayenne and salt.
6. Assemble — rice in bowls, topped with cucumber, sliced avocado, the chicken, kimchi,
   green onions, and a big handful of fried wontons. Spoon the yum yum sauce over the top.

## Notes

Sriracha works in place of the chili paste. Roast bell peppers and broccoli on a second
sheet pan to serve alongside. Coconut aminos sub for the tamari/soy sauce.

**Sesame chili crisp** (optional, for drizzling): cook 3/4 cup sesame or peanut oil with
8 cloves finely chopped garlic over medium heat until the garlic turns golden and crisp,
5–8 minutes. Stir in 3 tbsp sesame seeds and cook 1 minute. Pour the oil into a large bowl
and add 1–3 tbsp crushed red pepper flakes, 1 tbsp gochugaru (optional), 1/2 tsp paprika,
and 1/4–1/2 tsp cayenne. Season with fish sauce, tamari, or salt.
