## Instructions

1. Dice the red onion, green pepper, and jalapeño.
2. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks.
   Once it's cooked through, stir in the chili seasoning packet.
3. Transfer the beef to a crock pot and add the tomato sauce, diced tomatoes, chili beans,
   onion, green pepper, and jalapeño. Stir to combine.
4. Cook 3–4 hours on high, or 6–8 hours on low.

## Notes

80% ground beef gives a richer chili; 90% keeps it leaner. Leave the jalapeño seeds in for
more heat, or swap in mild chili beans and seasoning to dial it back. Great with shredded
cheddar, sour cream, or oyster crackers.
