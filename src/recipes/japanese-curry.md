## Instructions

1. Mince the garlic, chop the onion, and dice the carrot and potato. Dice the chicken thighs.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat and sauté the garlic and onion until aromatic.
3. Add the chicken and fish sauce. Sauté until lightly browned, then season with ground
   black pepper.
4. Add the carrots and potatoes and stir to coat.
5. Pour in the water, cover, and simmer 10–15 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
6. Add the curry cubes to the broth and stir carefully until dissolved. Keep cooking over
   low-medium heat about 10 minutes, until thickened. Season with salt if needed.

## Notes

Chicken is 400 g (about 0.9 lb). Use 3 cubes for a lighter sauce, 4 for a thicker, richer
one — S&B Golden Curry or House Foods Vermont Curry both work, in mild through hot. Serve
over steamed rice (not counted in the macros).
